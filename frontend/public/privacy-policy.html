EzBill

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This policy explains how ProDevOpz Solutions ("we", "us") handles data in the EzBill restaurant billing service. It covers our website (ezbill.jobsio.in) and our Android app (com.ezbill.app).

1. Data we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, mobile number and password. Passwords are stored as a hash, not as plain text.
  • Restaurant details: restaurant name, address, GSTIN, FSSAI number, UPI ID and logo.
  • Staff details: staff names, roles and login PINs.
  • Business records: menu items, tables, orders, bills, payment method used and amounts.
  • Customer details entered in orders: a customer's name or phone number, when the restaurant or customer provides them, such as for QR ordering or a receipt.
  • Photos you choose: your logo and menu photos.
  • Device and technical data: push notification token, and the IP address and device or browser details saved with consent records.
  • Consent records: your privacy, terms and marketing choices, and when you made them.

Restaurants are responsible for the customer details they enter and for having a valid reason to collect them.

3. How we use data

  • To run billing, orders, kitchen tickets, printing, QR ordering and reports.
  • To keep menus, tables and orders in sync across your staff's devices.
  • To sign you in and send password reset codes and important service messages.
  • To process subscription and other payments.
  • To read menu photos with AI, when you use that feature.
  • To give support, keep the service secure and meet legal and tax duties.
  • To send promotional messages, only if you opt in. You can turn this off in the app.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as needed with these service providers:

  • Razorpay: payment processing. We do not store card numbers or CVVs.
  • MongoDB Atlas: database hosting.
  • Render and Vercel: server and website hosting.
  • Brevo: sending emails, such as password reset codes.
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivering push notifications.
  • Google Gemini API: reading menu photos, only when you use the AI menu feature.

We may also share data when the law or a government authority requires it. Our providers may process data on servers outside India.

5. Security

Data between the app or website and our servers is sent over HTTPS, and access to a restaurant's data requires a login. No online service can promise complete security, so please keep your password and PINs private.

6. How long we keep data

We keep account and restaurant data while your account is active. After deletion, we keep bills and orders with customer names, customer phone numbers and notes removed, for as long as tax law requires (Section 36 of the CGST Act, 72 months). We may also keep a record of the deletion request and of past consent as legal proof.

7. Delete your account and data

In the app: open More, then Legal, Privacy & DPDP, then Privacy Controls, and tap Erase My Account & Personal Data.

Without the app: email contactprodevopz@gmail.com from your registered email address, with the subject "EzBill account deletion request". We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.

When a restaurant owner deletes an account, we delete the account and staff logins, restaurant profile, menu, categories, tables, notifications and subscription records. When a staff member deletes an account, only that staff login is deleted. Bills and orders are handled as explained in section 6.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and subject to applicable law, you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and raise a complaint. You can also export your data as a file from Privacy Controls in the app.

9. Children

EzBill is a business tool for adults and is not meant for children. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, please contact us so we can delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The latest version will always be on this page, with its updated date.
