Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about EzBill Restaurant POS? Here is everything you need to know.

💡 How fast can I set up EzBill for my restaurant?

You can get started in less than 3 minutes. Simply register an account, add your tables, upload your menu items (or use our AI Menu Scanner to photograph your physical menu), and start taking orders right away from your phone, tablet, laptop, or desktop.

🖨️ Does EzBill work with standard Bluetooth and USB thermal printers?

Yes! EzBill supports all standard 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) ESC/POS thermal printers. You can connect over Bluetooth, USB OTG, WiFi LAN, or print directly using your browser's native print dialog.

🧾 How does EzBill ensure 100% Float-Safe GST calculations?

Traditional POS systems perform floating-point division which results in fractions of a paise (e.g. ₹0.0034) causing end-of-day discrepancy. EzBill executes integer arithmetic in whole paise, ensuring exact CGST (2.5%), SGST (2.5%), and compliant round-offs every single bill.

⚡ Does EzBill work offline if my restaurant internet disconnects?

Yes! With our Local-First engine, table statuses, order modifications, and thermal bill prints process locally on your device in 0ms. When internet connectivity resumes, changes sync silently to your secure cloud database.

📱 Can I install EzBill as an app on iOS and Android?

Absolutely. EzBill is engineered as a modern Progressive Web App (PWA) with native mobile applications. You can add it to your iPhone or iPad home screen, Android device, or Windows desktop in 1-click without going through app store delays.
